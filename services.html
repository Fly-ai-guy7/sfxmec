Why Tensile Membrane?

Tensioned membranes achieve massive clear spans exceeding 100 meters without intermediate columns. They introduce sculptural organic curvature, dramatically reduce structural dead weight, and resist extreme thermal expansion.

Monumental Clear Spans

Natural Light & Glazing Alternative

Replaces heavy, brittle glass skylights. Architectural membranes reject 75% to 85% of solar heat gain while transmitting 9% to 18% of soft, glare-free daylight, significantly cutting artificial lighting and HVAC chiller bills.

85% Solar Heat Deflection

FEA & Deflection Engineering

Specialized non-linear large-displacement analysis using sophisticated form-finding algorithms. We calculate pretension fields, dynamic gust response, anti-ponding water drainage paths, and cable-net boundary equilibrium.

Zero Ponding Guarantee

Aluminum & Glass Facade Integration

Engineered interfaces between lightweight tensioned facades and conventional aluminum curtain walls. Provides building envelopes with second-skin solar mesh screens that drop perimeter wall surface temperatures by up to 15°C.

Second-Skin Thermal Mesh

GCC Municipal & Fire Compliance

Certified Stamped Engineering
Execution Methodology

Moving From Design to Realization

A disciplined four-phase delivery framework that ensures precision tolerances from initial sketch to final cable tensioning.

Phase 01

Form Finding & 3D Equilibrium

Parametric computational generation of minimal surfaces in pure tension. We balance architectural aesthetics with curvature ratios to eliminate stress concentrations and ponding pockets.

Parametric 3D Surface Model Curvature Radii Map
Phase 02

FEA Non-Linear Engineering

Rigorous simulations accounting for membrane orthotropic elasticity, creep compensation, thermal expansion under 50°C ambient, and wind gust uplift according to ASCE 7-16.

Reaction Force Schedules Boundary Steel Shop Drawings
Phase 03

CNC Patterning & Cleanroom Welding

High-precision compensation patterning transformed into flat cutting files. Automated CNC vacuum cutting followed by high-frequency (HF) molecular welding in temperature-controlled cleanrooms.

Computer-Compensated Patterns 100% Weld Strength Verification
Phase 04

Rigging, Tensioning & Commissioning

Deployment of certified installation riggers, mobile crane lifts, hydraulic jacking systems, and calibrated load cells to bring membrane panels to design pretension.

Pretension Log Certificate Turnkey As-Built Handover
