The Engineering Journey

Moving From Design To Realization

Tensile membranes derive their strength from geometric double curvature and internal prestress. Every SFXMEC project progresses through a rigorous 4-stage engineering lifecycle.

01

Form Finding & 3D Modeling

Computational physical equilibrium simulation. We determine minimal surface shapes where tension stress is uniformly distributed under initial prestress boundaries.

Rhino 3D Grasshopper Easy Tensile
02

Non-Linear FEA Analysis

Geometric non-linear finite element modeling testing dynamic wind uplift, thermal expansion (+55°C), desert dust ponding, and mast boundary stiffness.

ANSYS ASCE 7-16 DIN 4134
03

Biaxial Patterning & CNC Cut

Flattening 3D doubly-curved membrane panels into 2D cutting patterns with precision biaxial stretch compensation, followed by ultrasonic CNC cleanroom welding.

CNC Vacuum Bed HF Welding Biaxial Rig
04

Erection, Prestress & Testing

On-site rigging, mast hoisting, perimeter clamp detailing, and calibrated hydraulic jack pre-stressing monitored with real-time electronic load cells.

Hydraulic Jacks Load Cells IRATA Rope
