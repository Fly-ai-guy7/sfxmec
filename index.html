Marine & Coastal Engineering

Hypersaline Resilience & Flying Mast Architecture

Harbor marinas, yacht clubs, and coastal promenades across the Gulf face extreme atmospheric salt-spray, high ambient humidity, and UV exposure. Our marine-grade tensile systems deploy suspended Flying Mast mechanics and duplex stainless alloys built to thrive in coastal environments.

Flying Mast Floating Technology

The central compression mast is held entirely aloft by balanced upper and lower stay cables—floating with zero ground contact. This creates 100% unimpeded dockside clearance for marina boat slips, luxury yachts, and coastal vehicle turnarounds.

  • Zero Ground Footprint: Mast floats 4.5m above finished grade
  • Aerodynamic Stability: Anticlastic double curvature sheds gale wind gusts
  • Prestige Visual Form: As installed at private luxury seaside villas in Abu Dhabi

AISI 316 & 2205 Duplex Hardware

Conventional carbon steel rapidly bleeds rust in marine microclimates. We specify electro-polished AISI 316 and 2205 duplex marine stainless steel for all tension cables, swaged terminals, pinned shackles, and perimeter clamping plates.

  • ASTM B117 Tested: 3,000+ hours continuous salt-fog spray resistance
  • Zero Surface Staining: Protects brilliant white membranes from rust runoff
  • Dielectric Isolation: Teflon isolation pads prevent galvanic corrosion

Chemically Inert PTFE & PVDF Topcoats

Fluorinated membranes act as a non-stick shield against crystalline salt incrustation and coastal humidity. Ambient morning dew and seasonal rain naturally break down marine grime through low-surface-energy release.

  • 100% UV Impervious: Zero embrittlement or yellowing over 30+ year service
  • Sand Abrasion Shield: PVDF lacquers withstand desert dust storms
  • Non-Combustible (Class A): ASTM E108 compliant for commercial port terminals

Port Terminals, Ferry Hubs & Waterfront Drop-Offs

The Engineering & Execution Process

Engineering is the core of our industry. Critical shape and deflection analysis is a major consideration to ensure ponding cannot occur for runoff, sand, and wind loading under appropriate regional design standards.

01

Form Finding & 3D Modeling

Equilibrium surface generation. We calculate initial prestress distributions to ensure continuous double curvature (synclastic or anticlastic) preventing ponding.

Rhino 3D Grasshopper Easy Tensile
02

Deflection & Non-Linear FEA

Geometric non-linear finite element analysis simulating Middle Eastern wind load gusts (up to 160 km/h), thermal expansion, and runoff drainage channels.

ASCE 7-16 DIN 4134 Wind CFD
03

Cleanroom CNC Fabrication

Biaxial compensation flattening, vacuum table ultrasonic automated cutting, and high-frequency (HF) welding in controlled temperature & humidity cleanrooms.

CNC Cutting Bed HF Welding Peel Testing
04

Rigging, Tensioning & Erection

Certified on-site erection by master riggers using hydraulic tensioning jacks, precision boundary clamping, and real-time electronic load cell monitoring.

Hydraulic Jacks Load Cells Certified Rigging
