Our Heritage

We Support Your Idea Until It Becomes Reality

Structurflex Middle East Contracting LLC (SFXMEC) has been established in the MENA region since 2005. Over two decades of specialized focus, our multidisciplinary practice has completed landmark architectural commissions across the United Arab Emirates, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and Qatar.

The company founder holds distinguished international experience in the tensile membrane industry and has spearheaded a vast array of high-profile structures—from stadium canopies and airport concourses to bespoke residential flying masts and university courtyard shade systems.

Our operating philosophy is simple: we provide 100% turnkey responsibility from initial architectural form-finding and non-linear finite element analysis (FEA), through cleanroom membrane seaming, to crane-lift erection and certified commissioning.

In-House Engineering

Non-linear finite element modeling, solar radiation simulation, and certified wind tunnel deflection analysis.

Cleanroom Welding

Automated CNC plotting and high-frequency (HF) molecular seaming conforming to strict German DIN and ASTM tolerances.

Regional Compliance

Fully compliant with UAE Fire and Life Safety Codes, Saudi Building Code (SBC), and GCC municipal standards.

20+ Years Track Record

Over 250 completed tensile membrane installations, enduring extreme desert heat, humidity, and coastal sandstorms.
