Data Governance & Confidentiality

Privacy Policy & Technical Data Protection

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Structurflex Middle East Contracting LLC (SFXMEC)

1. Corporate Scope & Overview

Structurflex Middle East Contracting LLC (referred to as "SFXMEC", "we", "us", or "our") is a specialized tensile membrane contracting and structural engineering enterprise operating across the United Arab Emirates, Egypt, the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and Qatar.

This Privacy Policy explains how SFXMEC collects, processes, and protects personal and corporate data provided through our website (www.sfxmec.com), engineering consultation forms, and direct email communications.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information exclusively necessary to evaluate, calculate, and engineer architectural membrane scopes:

  • Contact Information: Full name, professional title, corporate affiliation, business email address, direct telephone and WhatsApp contact numbers.
  • Project Metadata: Proposed project geographical location, jurisdiction, architectural typology (e.g. school courtyard, car shade, atrium, facade, arena), estimated footprint in square meters, and timeline expectations.
  • Technical Submissions: CAD drawings, architectural renderings, site plans, structural tender packages, and specifications transmitted to our engineering desk for preliminary form-finding or budgetary quotations.
  • Technical Browsing Data: Standard server logs, IP addresses, browser types, and anonymous interaction metrics used to enhance platform performance.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

The information we collect is utilized strictly for professional business purposes:

  • Evaluating structural feasibility and preparing preliminary engineering briefs, tonnage schedules, and stamped commercial tenders.
  • Facilitating direct communication between our senior engineers in Dubai and Cairo and your project delivery team.
  • Executing Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and fulfilling legal obligations under construction contracting agreements in the GCC.
  • Maintaining operational records in compliance with regional commercial licensing standards.

4. Confidentiality of Architectural Plans & NDAs

We recognize that architectural concepts and proprietary structural specifications are valuable intellectual assets. All proprietary drawings, CAD models, and design files shared with SFXMEC are treated with strict confidentiality.

Technical materials are shared internally only with certified structural engineers, draftsmen, and estimators directly assigned to your tender. We routinely execute bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to formal bid preparation upon client request.

5. Compliance with Regional Regulations

Our data processing protocols comply with applicable privacy and data protection legislation across our operational territories, including:

  • United Arab Emirates: UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection (PDPL).
  • Kingdom of Saudi Arabia: KSA Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree M/19).
  • Egypt: Law No. 151 of 2020 on the Protection of Personal Data.

6. Data Security & Storage

SFXMEC deploys industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect technical and personal submissions from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Digital files are housed on secure cloud servers with encrypted transit (TLS/SSL) and restricted internal access.
